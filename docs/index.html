01 / THE PROBLEM

Summaries are not knowledge.

A paper reader that returns a polished abstract has answered the wrong question.

Research needs different answers: what does this paper claim, which experiment actually supports each claim, is the comparison fair, what remains unproven, how does it differ from the closest work — and what should you do next? Paper Research Skill encodes that discipline: read in passes, not in pages; build a claim graph, not a section summary; end every reading in a decision.

03 / READING MODES

Four depths. One decision.

Not every paper deserves the same hour. The skill picks exactly one mode — and stops early when triage says stop.

Pick a mode

$ read arXiv:2601.00001 --mode SCAN

triage:
  problem:  long-horizon agents can't reuse experience
  idea:     compressed skill memory + retrieval-gated planner
  result:   68.2 vs 64.1 on HotpotQA-hard
  relevance: high
  recommended_depth: RESEARCH

SCAN / minutes

Is it worth reading?

Title → abstract → Figure 1 → conclusion → main table. Ends in a forced three-sentence verdict and a keep/drop decision.

Show the verdict format

Existing XXX methods suffer from XXX. This paper proposes XXX, which solves it by XXX. On XXX benchmark it improves over XXX by XXX.

Most papers should be filtered out here. That is the point. 

minimal_pipeline:
  Trajectory → Compressor → Memory store
    → Retriever → Planner → Actor

new_arrow: Memory → Planner

novelty: "Compared with vanilla ReAct+RAG,
  they add a compressed skill-memory store
  with a retrieval-gated planner."

UNDERSTAND / ~30–60 min

How does the method work?

De-package the terminology into the simplest system diagram, then find the one arrow that is actually new — and the causal chain that says why it should work.

Show the causal chain

Problem → why prior methods fail → proposed mechanism → why it should work in principle → which experiment validates it.

If the novelty cannot be compressed into one sentence, it is not yet understood. 

critique:
  - dimension: baseline_fairness
    status: concern
    observation: "ours retrieves 10 docs,
      baselines 5"
    why_it_matters: "gain may be budget,
      not mechanism"
    resolving_experiment: "rerun strongest
      baseline at equal budget"

REVIEW / +critique

Is it credible?

Reviewer mode: every major claim is aligned with the experiment that tests it, then attacked along a fixed 12-dimension matrix.

Show the critique rule

Every criticism must be specific, evidence-grounded, and testable: Observation → Why it matters → Experiment that could resolve it. “More experiments are needed” is forbidden.

End-to-end wins with multiple simultaneous changes are INDIRECT evidence — never proof of the mechanism. 

research_hypotheses:
  - gap: "no equal-budget comparison of
      memory representations"
    hypothesis: "evidence-state memory beats
      raw-trajectory memory at fixed budget"
    minimal_experiment: "5-doc cap, 3 seeds,
      HotpotQA-hard"
    next_action: [COMPARE, USE_AS_BASELINE]

RESEARCH / hours

What does it mean for my work?

Overlap, difference, threat to novelty, reusable components — then grounded hypotheses in the form Gap → Hypothesis → Minimal experiment → Expected observation.

Show the long-term payoff

100 papers read this way become 100 falsifiable research hypotheses — a completely different asset from 100 summaries.

Reading must terminate in a decision: IGNORE · SAVE · CITE · COMPARE · USE_AS_BASELINE · REPRODUCE · INTEGRATE · INVESTIGATE.

04 / THE ARCHITECTURE

Workflow below. Judgment on top.

Three layers turn a reading prompt into a research instrument — plus optional multi-agent escalation when the paper earns it.

  1. LAYER 01

    Workflow

    Scan → Understand → Verify → Critique. The fixed scientific discipline that keeps the agent honest and cheap.

    10 steps, fixed
  2. LAYER 02

    Evidence State

    Claim ↔ Evidence ↔ Confidence ↔ Uncertainty. A living state that absorbs appendices, code, and author responses.

    vt → vt+1 updatable
  3. LAYER 03

    Research Agent

    Compare, challenge, connect, hypothesize. The open judgment space where new research directions appear.

    4 roles, on demand

Multi-agent escalation: SCAN → Reader · REVIEW → Reader + Experiment Critic · RESEARCH → Reader + Method Critic + Experiment Critic · conflicts → Research Judge. Escalation policy ↗
